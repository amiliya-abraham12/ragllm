Students are expected to attain 100% attendance, with a mandatory minimum of 75% required to be eligible for the end semester examination. A 2% relaxation in attendance is granted for menstrual leave, and a 5% relaxation is granted for students with disabilities. In exceptional cases, the Principal may grant condonation of attendance for students with attendance less than 75% but greater than or equal to 60%.
