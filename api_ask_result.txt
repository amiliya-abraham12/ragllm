To answer your question, according to the university document excerpt, the minimum attendance requirement for semester advancement is that students must achieve at least 25% average attendance in the current semester. (B. Tech. Regulation, 2024 APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, R 7.31, i)

Therefore, the answer to your question is:

The minimum attendance required for semester advancement is 25%.
